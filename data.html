What “underlying data” means here

The site publishes the derived decision-level and player-level datasets that directly support the article and leaderboards. Raw nflverse play-by-play is not duplicated inside this site; the source is publicly available from nflverse and the published decision ledger identifies every selected game and opportunity needed to trace the analysis.
