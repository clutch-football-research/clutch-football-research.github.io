Results · Methodology v1.2

Let the measures disagree.

CDCR tells you what happened. xCDCR estimates the difficulty. CAE compares observed and expected conversion. sCDCR standardizes the partially pooled estimate onto common opportunity footing.

Observed result

Burrow: 56.0%

14 conversions in 25 resolved career opportunities; observed CDCR rank #7 in the 25+ cohort.

Large-sample comparison

Brady: 56.6%

83 resolved opportunities; sCDCR point estimate 49.8%.

sCDCR validation status

Rank stability passed across the predeclared model variants. The held-out predictive comparison is mixed, with no clear predictive gain: Brier score is trivially better and log loss trivially worse than globally calibrated xCDCR. The site therefore does not claim that sCDCR improves prediction.
