Why this is different from a retrospective chart

A 2022 point is not today's model projected backward onto 2022. The 2022 point is rebuilt using only information that would have existed at the end of the 2022 season. The cohort, opportunity model, shrinkage and standardized estimate all evolve as new evidence arrives.
